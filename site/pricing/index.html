What is a Credit?

A single currency for global expansion.

1 Credit = 1 Market Scan

Use a credit to perform a comprehensive Rove Scan in one of our 30+ available territories. Get instant data on regulatory requirements, market size, and entry barriers.

1 Credit = 1 Product Secure

Share one product in one territory with verified retailers, distributors, and sales partners. Connect directly with the right buyers who can bring your product to market.

Membership Principles

  • Unused credits roll over to the next month as long as your membership is active.

  • If you cancel, any remaining credits are forfeited immediately, but you keep full platform access until the end of your billing period.
